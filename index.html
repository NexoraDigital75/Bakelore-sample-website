The Bakelore idea

Traditional lore.
Modern bake.

Bakelore describes its recipes as inspired by cherished flavours and memories of the ’90s, with a contemporary aesthetic and a focus on baked goods, savouries and desserts.

Why Bakelore

More than a bakery.

The brand describes itself as an experience built around nostalgia, warmth, friendship and the pleasure of sharing good food. Its range spans cakes, pastries, stuffed buns, burgers, pizzas, wraps, sandwiches, beverages and more.

The Banjara Hills outlet is listed by Bakelore as a flagship retail store with desserts, coffee, savoury food and cozy corners.
